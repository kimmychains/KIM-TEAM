EFFECTIVE HABITS / 04

Live by principles.

Build a life guided by values, responsibility and deliberate choices rather than simply following whatever happens around you.

Principles give your decisions direction.

Habits determine what you repeatedly do. Principles help determine why you do it. When your actions are guided by clear values, it becomes easier to make decisions even when circumstances change.

Foundations of Principled Living

01

Responsibility

Take ownership of your choices, commitments and responsibilities instead of constantly placing blame elsewhere.

02

Integrity

Let your actions remain consistent with what you believe is right, including when nobody is watching.

03

Discipline

Do what needs to be done even when motivation is temporary or circumstances are not ideal.

04

Respect

Treat yourself, other people and their time with consideration. Strong learning environments depend on mutual respect.

05

Growth

Remain willing to learn, receive correction and improve. Being unfinished is not the same as being incapable.

06

Purpose

Know what you are working toward so that your daily choices connect to something larger than immediate comfort.

Before you act, pause and think.

Good decisions do not always begin with complicated analysis. Sometimes they begin with a few honest questions about whether an action agrees with your responsibilities and values.

Ask yourself:

  • Is this consistent with my values?
  • What are the consequences of this choice?
  • Am I acting deliberately or simply reacting?
  • Does this support the person I want to become?
  • Am I willing to take responsibility for the result?

Put Principles Into Practice

01

Keep Your Word

Treat commitments seriously and communicate honestly when circumstances change.

02

Do the Work

Replace endless intention with consistent action toward meaningful responsibilities.

03

Accept Correction

Use feedback as information that can help you improve rather than automatically treating it as an attack.

04

Review Yourself

Regularly examine your choices and identify what you should continue, change or improve.

Personal Reflection

A principled life is built through repeated decisions. Take a moment to examine your own direction.

  • What principles are most important to me?
  • Which of my daily habits support those principles?
  • Which habits conflict with the person I want to become?
  • Where do I need greater discipline?
  • What is one decision I can make differently today?

Build your habits. Build your character.

Effective habits are not only about getting more done. They are also about becoming more intentional about how you learn, work and live.
