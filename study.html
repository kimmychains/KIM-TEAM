Effective Habits / 01

Learn how to study.

Studying is more than sitting with a book. It is a deliberate process of understanding, remembering, practising and applying knowledge.

Study smarter. Understand deeper.

Effective studying begins with knowing what you are trying to learn and choosing methods that make your brain actively work with the material. Instead of measuring study by hours alone, focus on understanding, recall, practice and improvement.

Core Principles

Four ideas behind effective study

Strong learning is built around active engagement, not simply exposure to information.

01

Understand

Ask what an idea means, why it matters, and how it connects to what you already know.

02

Recall

Close your notes and try to reproduce what you learned from memory.

03

Practise

Use questions, problems and applications to turn knowledge into usable ability.

04

Review

Return to important material regularly instead of relying entirely on last-minute revision.

05

Connect

Link new ideas to examples, previous lessons and real situations.

06

Reflect

Identify what you understand, what you do not, and what needs another attempt.

Build a study method that works.

Active Recall

Test yourself instead of repeatedly reading the same notes.

Practice Questions

Use questions to discover whether you can actually apply what you learned.

Spaced Review

Revisit important material over time rather than depending only on one long session.

Explain It

Explain an idea in your own words to reveal gaps in your understanding.
